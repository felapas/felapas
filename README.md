## 📚 Publications & Featured Projects

### 🎵 Musical Creativity Index — LAMIR 2026

**First author | Paper accepted at the Latin American Music Information Retrieval Workshop (LAMIR 2026)**

Research on out-of-distribution creativity in symbolic music generation. We adapt the Creativity Index to evaluate structural uniqueness in music generated by models such as MIDI-GPT and Aria, comparing model-generated continuations with human compositions across different musical domains.

- Developed a reproducible evaluation benchmark with 300 prompts.
- Built the **BiaCorpus**, a Brazilian MIDI dataset with 2,277 eligible sources.
- Released the code, benchmark data, and dataset publicly.

**[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/felapas/biacorpus-mci)** | **[BiaCorpus on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/felpdamasceno/BiaCorpus)**

### ⚽ WC-IMPACT — FAME 2026

**Paper accepted at Football Analytics Modelling & Experience (FAME 2026)**

A reproducible causal inference study investigating how participation in the 2022 FIFA World Cup affected players' subsequent utilization in Europe's top five football leagues.

The project combines data engineering, statistical modeling, causal inference, and robustness analysis using **Python and DuckDB**.

**[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/felapas/WC-IMPACT)**

### 🤖 AI-Generated Video & Audience Interaction Research

**Ongoing research | NLP, Generative AI, and Data Engineering**

Research investigating differences between human-created and AI-generated video content on **YouTube and TikTok**.

Current work includes video and comment data acquisition, transcript processing, multilingual text embeddings, semantic similarity analysis, and the design of scalable evaluation pipelines.

### 🏆 Reply Brasil Hackathon 2026 — 1st Place

Developed, as part of a team, a computer vision system for detecting, tracking, counting, and estimating the volume of batteries on industrial recycling conveyor belts.

The solution combined instance segmentation, temporal tracking, geometric calibration, uncertainty estimation, and a React-based interface.

**[Project Repository](https://github.com/DataCaio/Hackathon-reply)**
